# INC-0181 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00320 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00367 | **SKU:** HBLC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
2 of 5 pallets of HBLC-001 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00367 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline

- **2026-10-12 00:10** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Supplier label printer ran out of ribbon mid-shift; pallets left the plant unlabeled.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation. Receiving lead verified case counts against ASN before relabeling.
