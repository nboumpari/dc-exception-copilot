# INC-0190 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00336 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00376 | **SKU:** HBLC-002 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 6 pallets of HBLC-002 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Splashless 3.78 L) on RCV-00376 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Root cause
Label exposed to rain during trailer loading at origin; ink smeared.

## Corrective and preventive actions
