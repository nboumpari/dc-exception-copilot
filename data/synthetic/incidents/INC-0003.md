# INC-0003 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00006 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00008 | **SKU:** DISH-003 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 6 pallets of DISH-003 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Sensitive 650 mL) on RCV-00008 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-07-21 10:54** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00008).
- **2026-07-21 10:54** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-07-22 20:14** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label placed over a pallet wrap seam, causing wrinkles across the GS1-128 symbol.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Barcode verifier grade logged and sent to supplier. Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4

Recurring pattern for this partner; tracked in the monthly partner review.
