# INC-0059 - Incompatible hazmat classes planned on one trailer

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00087 | **Severity:** High | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** shipment SHP-00270 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
Dock check on SHP-00270 to ProClean Janitorial Supply found class 3 and class 5.1 materials (not limited quantity) planned on the same trailer without separation.

## Timeline
- **2026-08-08 07:30** - Exception detected and logged (shipment SHP-00270).
- **2026-08-08 07:30** - Ravi Shah (Hazmat Shipping Clerk) assigned as owner.
- **2026-08-08 21:15** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Load plan combined two professional orders without running the segregation check.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Pallets separated within the trailer so commingling could not occur in a leak, with corrosive liquids not loaded above or adjacent to oxidizers. Load re-planned: incompatible pallets moved to a second trailer.
