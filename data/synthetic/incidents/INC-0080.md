# INC-0080 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00128 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved

## Summary
1 of 7 pallets of HBLC-002 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Splashless 3.78 L) on RCV-00131 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.
