# Safety Data Sheet - CleanPro Hydrogen Peroxide 35%

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** CleanPro Hydrogen Peroxide 35% (20 L drum)
- **Product family code:** HPOX
- **Recommended use:** Hydrogen peroxide 35% (professional)
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Oxidizing liquid (H272: may intensify fire; oxidizer)
- Acute toxicity oral, Category 4 (H302)
- Serious eye damage, Category 1 (H318)
- STOT single exposure, Category 3 (H335)

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Get medical attention if irritation or breathing difficulty occurs.
- **Skin:** Immediately remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention.
- **Eyes:** Immediately rinse with water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if easy. Get immediate medical attention.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting. Get immediate medical attention.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Oxidizer; decomposes releasing oxygen, which intensifies fire. Flood with water. Containers may rupture from oxygen pressure.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Dilute with large amounts of water. Do not absorb with combustible materials. Keep away from corrosives bay H4 and flammables H2.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H3
(Oxidizer bay). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Combustible and organic materials, flammable liquids, reducing agents, bases (caustic stripper), metals and metal salts (catalytic decomposition), hypochlorites.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Face shield, goggles, PVC or neoprene gloves, apron.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Clear colorless liquid; pH 2-3; vented drum closures.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Combustible and organic materials, flammable liquids, reducing agents, bases (caustic stripper), metals and metal salts (catalytic decomposition), hypochlorites.

## 11. Toxicological information
Causes serious eye damage; irritates skin and airways.

## 12. Ecological information
Decomposes to water and oxygen; harmful at high concentration.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
