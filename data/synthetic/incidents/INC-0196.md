**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00350 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** open

2 of 8 pallets of DISH-003 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Sensitive 650 mL) on RCV-00387 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.
