# INC-0153 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Exception:** EXC-00264 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00310 | **SKU:** DISH-002 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 5 pallets of DISH-002 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Original 650 mL) on RCV-00310 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.
