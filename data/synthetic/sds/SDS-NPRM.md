# Safety Data Sheet - Silkara Nail Polish Remover (Acetone)

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Silkara Nail Polish Remover (Acetone) (200 mL)
- **Product family code:** NPRM
- **Recommended use:** Nail polish remover
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Flammable liquid, Category 2 (H225)
- Eye irritation, Category 2A (H319)
- Specific target organ toxicity - single exposure, Category 3 (H336: may cause drowsiness or dizziness)

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. If drowsiness or dizziness occurs, get medical advice.
- **Skin:** Rinse with water. Remove contaminated clothing away from ignition sources.
- **Eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. If irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Highly flammable; vapors heavier than air. Use alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical or CO2.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Remove ignition sources; ventilate; absorb with non-combustible absorbent.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H2
(Flammable liquids room). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Strong oxidizers (hydrogen peroxide, hypochlorites), strong acids, strong bases.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Goggles; butyl gloves for prolonged contact.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Clear volatile liquid; flash point approx. -20 C (closed cup).

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Strong oxidizers (hydrogen peroxide, hypochlorites), strong acids, strong bases.

## 11. Toxicological information
Vapors cause drowsiness at high concentration.

## 12. Ecological information
Readily biodegradable.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
