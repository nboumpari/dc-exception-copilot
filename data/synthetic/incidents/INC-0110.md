# INC-0110 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00180 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00205 | **SKU:** HBLC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 8 pallets of HBLC-001 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00205 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline
- **2026-09-03 16:17** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00205).
- **2026-09-03 16:17** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-09-04 15:03** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Supplier label printer ran out of ribbon mid-shift; pallets left the plant unlabeled.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation. Added supplier to enhanced inbound inspection list for 30 days.

- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §2.2.1

Earlier escalation would have reduced the impact.
