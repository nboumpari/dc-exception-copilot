# Safety Data Sheet - AquaShock Pool Shock (Calcium Hypochlorite)

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** AquaShock Pool Shock (Calcium Hypochlorite) (454 g)
- **Product family code:** PSHK
- **Recommended use:** Pool shock (calcium hypochlorite), consumer pack
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Oxidizing solid, Category 2 (H272: may intensify fire; oxidizer)
- Acute toxicity oral, Category 4 (H302)
- Skin corrosion, Category 1B (H314)
- Hazardous to the aquatic environment, acute, Category 1 (H400)
- Supplemental: contact with acids liberates toxic gas

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Get medical attention if irritation or breathing difficulty occurs.
- **Skin:** Immediately remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention.
- **Eyes:** Immediately rinse with water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if easy. Get immediate medical attention.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting. Get immediate medical attention.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Oxidizer: intensifies fire and can ignite combustibles. Use large amounts of water only. Do not use dry chemical or foam.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Sweep up into a clean, dry, dedicated container. Do not use combustible absorbents. Do not return spilled material to the original container.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H3
(Oxidizer bay). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: **Trichloroisocyanuric acid (TCCA) and other chlorinated isocyanurates - mixing can cause fire or explosion.** Acids, ammonia, organic materials, flammable liquids (ethanol, acetone), reducing agents, small amounts of water.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Goggles, face shield, rubber gloves; dust respirator for spills.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
White granules, chlorine odor; solution pH 10-11.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** **Trichloroisocyanuric acid (TCCA) and other chlorinated isocyanurates - mixing can cause fire or explosion.** Acids, ammonia, organic materials, flammable liquids (ethanol, acetone), reducing agents, small amounts of water.

## 11. Toxicological information
Corrosive; harmful if swallowed; dust irritates airways.

## 12. Ecological information
Very toxic to aquatic life.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
