# INC-0095 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00152 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00167 | **SKU:** BLCC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 1 pallets of BLCC-001 (CleanPro Concentrated Bleach 12.5% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00167 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-08-26 06:48** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00167).
- **2026-08-26 06:48** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-08-26 22:13** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label placed over a pallet wrap seam, causing wrinkles across the GS1-128 symbol.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label. Barcode verifier grade logged and sent to supplier.

- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4
