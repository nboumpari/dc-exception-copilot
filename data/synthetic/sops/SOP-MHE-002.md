# SOP-MHE-002 Forklift Battery Charging

## 1. Purpose
Charge and change lead-acid forklift batteries safely.

## 2. Charging area (29 CFR 1910.178(g))
- Battery charging is done only in the designated charging area next to door 24.
- The area has facilities for flushing and neutralizing spilled electrolyte, fire protection,
  protection of charging equipment from damage by trucks, and ventilation to disperse fumes
  from gassing batteries.
- No smoking, open flames or sparks in the charging area.

## 3. Charging steps
1. Park the truck in the charging bay; forks lowered, controls neutral, power off, brakes set.
2. Check the battery for damage and electrolyte leaks before connecting.
3. Connect the charger only when it is switched off.
4. Keep vent caps in place; keep the battery cover open during charging for ventilation.

## 4. Changing batteries
Use the battery hoist only. A reinstalled battery must be properly positioned and secured in
the truck (1910.178(g)(5)).

## 5. Electrolyte spill
Use the neutralizer in the charging area spill kit, wear face shield and acid gloves, and log
a `LEAK_SPILL` exception with zone "D".
