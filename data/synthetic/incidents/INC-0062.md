# INC-0062 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00095 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00096 | **SKU:** BLCC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 4 pallets of BLCC-001 (CleanPro Concentrated Bleach 12.5% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00096 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-08-11 09:56** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00096).
- **2026-08-11 09:56** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-08-13 22:36** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label placed over a pallet wrap seam, causing wrinkles across the GS1-128 symbol.

## Corrective and preventive actions
SSCC keyed manually from human-readable text after two-person verification. Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4

Recurring pattern for this partner; tracked in the monthly partner review.
