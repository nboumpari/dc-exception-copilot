# INC-0054 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00081 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00087 | **SKU:** DRNO-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 7 pallets of DRNO-001 (CleanPro Drain Opener Gel Max Gel 1 L) on RCV-00087 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline

- **2026-08-07 13:15** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-08-08 12:52** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Barcode printed with low contrast (worn print head).

## Corrective and preventive actions
Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label. Barcode verifier grade logged and sent to supplier.
