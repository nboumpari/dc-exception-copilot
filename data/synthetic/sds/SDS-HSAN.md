# Safety Data Sheet - Puro Hand Sanitizer Gel 70% Ethanol

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Puro Hand Sanitizer Gel 70% Ethanol (500 mL)
- **Product family code:** HSAN
- **Recommended use:** Hand sanitizer gel (70% ethanol)
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Flammable liquid, Category 2 (H225: Highly flammable liquid and vapor)
- Eye irritation, Category 2A (H319)

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. If drowsiness or dizziness occurs, get medical advice.
- **Skin:** Rinse with water. Remove contaminated clothing away from ignition sources.
- **Eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. If irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Highly flammable; vapors may travel to ignition source. Use alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical or CO2.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Remove ignition sources. Absorb with non-combustible absorbent; use non-sparking tools.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H2
(Flammable liquids room). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Strong oxidizers (pool shock, chlorine tablets, hydrogen peroxide), strong acids.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Goggles; nitrile gloves for spills.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Clear gel, alcohol odor; flash point approx. 21 C (closed cup).

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Strong oxidizers (pool shock, chlorine tablets, hydrogen peroxide), strong acids.

## 11. Toxicological information
Eye irritant. Ingestion of large amounts causes intoxication.

## 12. Ecological information
Ethanol is readily biodegradable.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
