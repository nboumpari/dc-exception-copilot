# Safety Data Sheet - Puro Ethanol 96% Sanitizer Base

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Puro Ethanol 96% Sanitizer Base (20 L drum)
- **Product family code:** ETHB
- **Recommended use:** Ethanol 96% sanitizer base (professional)

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. If drowsiness or dizziness occurs, get medical advice.
- **Skin:** Rinse with water. Remove contaminated clothing away from ignition sources.
- **Eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. If irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Highly flammable. Use alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical or CO2. Cool drums with water spray.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Remove ignition sources. Dike and absorb with non-combustible absorbent. Prevent entry to drains.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H2
(Flammable liquids room). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Strong oxidizers (calcium hypochlorite, TCCA, hydrogen peroxide), strong acids, alkali metals.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Goggles, nitrile gloves; respirator in confined spaces.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Clear liquid; flash point approx. 14 C (closed cup).

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Strong oxidizers (calcium hypochlorite, TCCA, hydrogen peroxide), strong acids, alkali metals.

## 11. Toxicological information
Eye irritant; CNS depression at high vapor concentration.

## 12. Ecological information
Readily biodegradable.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
