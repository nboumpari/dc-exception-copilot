# INC-0100 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00161 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00183 | **SKU:** HBLC-002 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 5 pallets of HBLC-002 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Splashless 3.78 L) on RCV-00183 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline
- **2026-08-31 10:23** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00183).
- **2026-08-31 10:23** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-09-02 23:06** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label applied to stretch-wrap and torn off during transit.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Added supplier to enhanced inbound inspection list for 30 days. Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation.

- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §2.2.1

The issue was caught by the control designed for it; no change to the control needed.
