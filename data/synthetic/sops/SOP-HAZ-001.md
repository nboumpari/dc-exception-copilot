# SOP-HAZ-001 Hazardous Materials Storage and Segregation in the Warehouse

## 1. Purpose
Store hazardous materials so that a leak or fire in one class cannot react with another.

## 2. Hazard classes stored at DC-COL1
| Class | Examples in our catalog | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Flammable gas (aerosols) | Air freshener, antiperspirant spray | H1 |
| 2.2 Non-flammable gas (aerosols) | Furniture polish aerosol | H1 |
| 3 Flammable liquid | Hand sanitizer gel, nail polish remover, ethanol 96% base | H2 |
| 5.1 Oxidizer | Pool shock, chlorine tablets (TCCA), hydrogen peroxide 35% | H3 |
| 8 Corrosive | Drain opener, concentrated bleach, caustic floor stripper | H4 |
| 9 Miscellaneous | Electric toothbrush (lithium ion battery in equipment) | B |

## 3. Segregation rules inside the building
1. **Oxidizers (5.1) never share a bay with flammable liquids (3) or corrosive liquids (8).**
   Zone H3 is separated from H2 and H4 by at least 6 m and a spill curb.
2. Corrosive liquids (8) are never stored on rack levels above oxidizers or flammables.
3. Chlorine-releasing products (calcium hypochlorite, TCCA, hypochlorite solutions) are kept
   away from acids and ammonia products. Mixing produces toxic chlorine or chloramine gas.
4. **Calcium hypochlorite (pool shock) and trichloroisocyanuric acid (TCCA chlorine tablets)
   are both class 5.1 but must never touch.** Mixing them can cause fire or explosion. They are
   stored in separate H3 bays (H3 aisle 01 = calcium hypochlorite, H3 aisle 02 = TCCA) and spills
   are collected into separate dedicated containers.
5. Aerosols stay inside the H1 cage; a ruptured aerosol can become a projectile in a fire.
6. These rules apply to **all** package sizes, including limited-quantity consumer packs.
   The limited-quantity exception changes transport rules (SOP-HAZ-003), not how we store.

## 4. Why the building rule is stricter than the transport rule
The 49 CFR 177.848 segregation table governs loading on a transport vehicle and applies to
packages that must be labeled or placarded. In storage we hold far larger quantities for longer,
so the DC applies class separation to every hazmat package regardless of the transport exception.

## 5. Safety Data Sheets
SDS for every hazardous product are available in the hazmat office and electronically on the
zone tablets. They must be readily accessible to employees during each work shift
(29 CFR 1910.1200(g)(8)). Before handling an unfamiliar hazmat product, read SDS sections
2 (hazards), 7 (handling and storage) and 10 (incompatible materials).

## 6. Daily checks (shift supervisor)
- No hazmat pallet outside its H-zone (except zone Q).
- Spill kits in H1-H4 complete and sealed.
- No leaking or bulging containers.
- Eyewash station in H4 tested weekly and logged.
