# INC-0050 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00077 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00084 | **SKU:** BLCC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
2 of 8 pallets of BLCC-001 (CleanPro Concentrated Bleach 12.5% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00084 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline

## Root cause
Supplier label printer ran out of ribbon mid-shift; pallets left the plant unlabeled.

## Corrective and preventive actions
