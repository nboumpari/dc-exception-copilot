# INC-0176 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00309 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00353 | **SKU:** DRNO-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 4 pallets of DRNO-001 (CleanPro Drain Opener Gel Max Gel 1 L) on RCV-00353 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline
- **2026-10-06 09:49** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00353).
- **2026-10-06 09:49** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-10-09 03:32** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Supplier label printer ran out of ribbon mid-shift; pallets left the plant unlabeled.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Supplier quality notice issued; supplier asked for 8D corrective action report. Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §2.2.1

## Lessons learned
The issue was caught by the control designed for it; no change to the control needed.
