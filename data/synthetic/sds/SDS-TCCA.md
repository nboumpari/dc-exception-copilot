# Safety Data Sheet - AquaShock Chlorine Tablets (TCCA)

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** AquaShock Chlorine Tablets (TCCA) (20 kg pail)
- **Product family code:** TCCA
- **Recommended use:** Chlorine tablets (trichloroisocyanuric acid), professional
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Oxidizing solid, Category 2 (H272)
- Acute toxicity oral, Category 4 (H302)
- Eye irritation, Category 2A (H319)
- STOT single exposure, Category 3 (H335: respiratory irritation)
- Hazardous to the aquatic environment, acute 1 / chronic 1 (H400, H410)
- Supplemental: contact with acids liberates toxic gas

## 3. Composition / information on ingredients
| Component | CAS number | % (w/w) |
|---|---|---|
| Trichloroisocyanuric acid | 87-90-1 | >95 |

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Get medical attention if irritation or breathing difficulty occurs.
- **Skin:** Immediately remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention.
- **Eyes:** Immediately rinse with water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if easy. Get immediate medical attention.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting. Get immediate medical attention.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Oxidizer. When wetted with small amounts of water may produce nitrogen trichloride (explosive). Use large amounts of water.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Collect into a clean, dry, dedicated container. Keep separate from calcium hypochlorite waste.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H3
(Oxidizer bay). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: **Calcium hypochlorite and other hypochlorites - mixing can cause fire or explosion.** Ammonia and ammonium salts, nitrogen-containing compounds, small amounts of water, reducing agents, organics, flammable liquids.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Goggles, rubber gloves, dust respirator.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
White 3-inch tablets, chlorine odor; solution pH 2.7-3.3.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** **Calcium hypochlorite and other hypochlorites - mixing can cause fire or explosion.** Ammonia and ammonium salts, nitrogen-containing compounds, small amounts of water, reducing agents, organics, flammable liquids.

## 11. Toxicological information
Harmful if swallowed; respiratory irritant.

## 12. Ecological information
Very toxic to aquatic life with long lasting effects.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
