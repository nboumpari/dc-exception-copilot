# INC-0106 - Incompatible hazmat classes planned on one trailer

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00172 | **Severity:** High | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** shipment SHP-00586 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
Dock check on SHP-00586 to AquaPool Supply found class 5.1 and class 8 materials (not limited quantity) planned on the same trailer without separation.

## Root cause
