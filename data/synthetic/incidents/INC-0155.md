# INC-0155 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00270 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00312 | **SKU:** DISH-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 6 pallets of DISH-001 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Lemon 650 mL) on RCV-00312 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-09-28 11:15** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00312).
- **2026-09-28 11:15** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-09-29 22:13** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Barcode quiet zone violated by a supplier handwritten note.

## Corrective and preventive actions
SSCC keyed manually from human-readable text after two-person verification. Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4

## Lessons learned
The issue was caught by the control designed for it; no change to the control needed.
