# Safety Data Sheet - Puro Household Bleach 6%

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Puro Household Bleach 6% (3.78 L)
- **Product family code:** HBLC
- **Recommended use:** Household bleach (6% sodium hypochlorite)
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Skin irritation, Category 2 (H315)
- Serious eye damage, Category 1 (H318)
- Supplemental: contact with acids liberates toxic gas

## 3. Composition / information on ingredients
| Component | CAS number | % (w/w) |
|---|---|---|
| Sodium hypochlorite | 7681-52-9 | 5-7 |
| Sodium hydroxide | 1310-73-2 | <1 |
| Sodium chloride | 7647-14-5 | 1-5 |
| Water | 7732-18-5 | 85-95 |

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Get medical attention if irritation or breathing difficulty occurs.
- **Skin:** Immediately remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention.
- **Eyes:** Immediately rinse with water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Remove contact lenses if easy. Get immediate medical attention.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting. Get immediate medical attention.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Not flammable, but decomposes when heated releasing chlorine. Cool containers with water.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Dilute small spills with water and absorb with inert absorbent. Never mix with acid or ammonia cleaners.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone A
(Ambient racking). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Acids (releases chlorine gas), ammonia and ammonia-containing cleaners (releases chloramines), oxidizable organics, metals.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Chemical goggles and nitrile gloves for spills.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Clear pale yellow liquid, chlorine odor; pH 11-12.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Acids (releases chlorine gas), ammonia and ammonia-containing cleaners (releases chloramines), oxidizable organics, metals.

## 11. Toxicological information
Eye damage on direct contact. Inhalation of mists irritates airways.

## 12. Ecological information
Toxic to aquatic life before dilution and neutralization.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.

## 14. Transport information
Not regulated as a hazardous material for transport (US DOT).

## 15. Regulatory information
Classified according to OSHA Hazard Communication Standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200.
