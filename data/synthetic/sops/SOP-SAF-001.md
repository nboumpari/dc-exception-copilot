# SOP-SAF-001 Fire Safety - Extinguishers and Exit Routes

## 1. Purpose
Keep fire extinguishers ready and exit routes usable at all times.

## 2. Portable fire extinguishers (29 CFR 1910.157)
| Requirement | Rule | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Distance, Class A (ordinary combustibles: cartons, pallets) | Max 75 ft (22.9 m) travel to an extinguisher | 1910.157(d)(2) |
| Distance, Class B (flammable liquids: zone H2, aerosols H1) | Max 50 ft (15.2 m) travel to an extinguisher | 1910.157(d)(4) |
| Visual inspection | Monthly | 1910.157(e)(2) |
| Maintenance check | Annually, date recorded | 1910.157(e)(3) |
| Employee training | On hire and at least annually | 1910.157(g) |

At DC-COL1 extinguishers are checked monthly by the EHS coordinator; the tag is initialed and
dated. A missing or discharged extinguisher is replaced within the same shift.

## 3. Exit routes (29 CFR 1910.37)
1. Exit routes are kept free and unobstructed - no pallets, carts or materials, even
   temporarily.
2. Where the direction to the nearest exit is not obvious, signs show the direction of travel.
3. Exit doors are never locked from the inside during working hours.

## 4. Hazmat zones
Fires involving oxidizers (zone H3) can be intensified by the product itself. Staff do not
fight fires in H3; they evacuate and call emergency services.
