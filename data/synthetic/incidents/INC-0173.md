# INC-0173 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00304 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00349 | **SKU:** DISH-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 3 pallets of DISH-001 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Lemon 650 mL) on RCV-00349 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline
- **2026-10-05 08:26** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00349).
- **2026-10-05 08:26** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-10-07 06:23** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Supplier applied only a shipping label; logistic label step skipped during peak.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Added supplier to enhanced inbound inspection list for 30 days. Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §2.2.1

## Lessons learned
The issue was caught by the control designed for it; no change to the control needed.
