# INC-0125 - Incompatible hazmat classes planned on one trailer

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00213 | **Severity:** High | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** shipment SHP-00704 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
Dock check on SHP-00704 to ProClean Janitorial Supply found class 3 and class 5.1 materials (not limited quantity) planned on the same trailer without separation.

## Timeline

- **2026-09-12 13:08** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
New planner unaware that limited-quantity exception does not apply to bulk pails/drums.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Pallets separated within the trailer so commingling could not occur in a leak, with corrosive liquids not loaded above or adjacent to oxidizers. Hazmat shipping clerk refresher briefing on 177.848 segregation table.
