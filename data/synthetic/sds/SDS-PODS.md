# Safety Data Sheet - Brightwash Laundry Detergent Pods

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Brightwash Laundry Detergent Pods (42 ct, 81 ct)
- **Product family code:** PODS
- **Recommended use:** Household unit-dose laundry detergent
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Warning

- Skin irritation, Category 2 (H315)
- Eye irritation, Category 2A (H319)

## 3. Composition / information on ingredients
| Component | CAS number | % (w/w) |
|---|---|---|
| Alcohols, C9-11, ethoxylated | 68439-46-3 | 20-30 |
| Sodium dodecylbenzenesulfonate | 25155-30-0 | 15-25 |
| Propylene glycol | 57-55-6 | 5-10 |
| Glycerol | 56-81-5 | 5-10 |
| Polyvinyl alcohol (film) | 9002-89-5 | 1-5 |

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air if symptoms occur.
- **Skin:** Rinse with water. Get medical advice if irritation persists.
- **Eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses if easy. If irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth and drink a glass of water. Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center if unwell.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Not flammable. Use media suitable for surrounding fire.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Collect crushed pouches in a dry container. Keep away from water to avoid foaming.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone B
(Fast-pick). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Water/moisture (film dissolves); strong oxidizers.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
Gloves and goggles when handling crushed product.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Liquid-filled water-soluble pouches; pH 7-8; not flammable.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Water/moisture (film dissolves); strong oxidizers.

## 11. Toxicological information
Concentrated detergent; eye irritant. Keep out of reach of children.

## 12. Ecological information
Surfactants are readily biodegradable.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.

## 14. Transport information
Not regulated as a hazardous material for transport (US DOT).

## 15. Regulatory information
Classified according to OSHA Hazard Communication Standard, 29 CFR 1910.1200.
