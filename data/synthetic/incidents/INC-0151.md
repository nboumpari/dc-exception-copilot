# INC-0151 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00258 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved

## Summary
2 of 8 pallets of DRNO-001 (CleanPro Drain Opener Gel Max Gel 1 L) on RCV-00296 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.
