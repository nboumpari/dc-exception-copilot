# INC-0171 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00300 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00343 | **SKU:** HBLC-002 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
2 of 8 pallets of HBLC-002 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Splashless 3.78 L) on RCV-00343 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-10-05 05:10** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00343).
- **2026-10-05 05:10** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-10-07 09:59** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label placed over a pallet wrap seam, causing wrinkles across the GS1-128 symbol.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Shared GS1 label placement guidance with supplier. Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label.

- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4
