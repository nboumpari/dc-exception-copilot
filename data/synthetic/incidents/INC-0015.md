# INC-0015 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00032 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00045 | **SKU:** DISH-003 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
2 of 3 pallets of DISH-003 (Puro Dishwashing Liquid Sensitive 650 mL) on RCV-00045 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-07-29 04:59** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00045).
- **2026-07-29 04:59** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-07-29 20:10** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Barcode printed with low contrast (worn print head).

## Corrective and preventive actions
Relabeled pallet with DC-printed GS1 logistic label. SSCC keyed manually from human-readable text after two-person verification.

## References
- Governing procedure: SOP-REC-002
- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4

## Lessons learned
Earlier escalation would have reduced the impact.
