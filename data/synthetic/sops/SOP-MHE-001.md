# SOP-MHE-001 Forklift Operation and Operator Training

## 1. Purpose
Make sure only trained and evaluated operators drive powered industrial trucks (forklifts,
reach trucks, pallet jacks) and that they drive safely.

## 2. Who may operate
Only employees who completed the DC training program **and** passed the practical evaluation.
Trainees may only drive under direct supervision of a qualified trainer, in an area where
they do not endanger others (29 CFR 1910.178(l)(2)).

## 3. Training program (29 CFR 1910.178(l))
Training combines formal instruction, practical training and an evaluation of the operator's
performance in the workplace. Content includes truck-related topics (controls, capacity,
stability) and workplace topics such as pedestrian traffic, narrow aisles, ramps and
hazardous (classified) locations - at DC-COL1 this includes the H2 flammable liquids room.

## 4. Evaluation and refresher training
- Each operator's performance is evaluated **at least once every three years**
  (1910.178(l)(4)(iii)).
- Refresher training is required when the operator:
  - was observed operating unsafely;
  - was involved in an accident or **near-miss**;
  - received an evaluation showing unsafe operation;
  - is assigned to a different type of truck; or
  - a workplace condition changes in a way that could affect safe operation
  (1910.178(l)(4)(ii)).
- At DC-COL1, every `FORKLIFT_NEAR_MISS` exception automatically creates a refresher training
  task for the operator involved, to be completed within 7 days.

## 5. Driving rules
1. Look in the direction of travel and keep a clear view of the path (1910.178(n)(6)). If the
   load blocks the view, travel in reverse.
2. Slow down and sound the horn at cross aisles and wherever vision is obstructed
   (1910.178(n)(4)).
3. Never travel with the load raised.
4. When leaving the truck unattended: forks fully lowered, controls neutral, power off, brakes
   set (1910.178(m)(5)(i)).
5. Keep aisles clear - never stage pallets in marked aisles (1910.176(a)).
6. Pedestrians are not allowed in forklift-only aisles in zones A and H1-H4.

## 6. Hazmat zones
Only trucks approved for the area may enter H2. In H3/H4, report any contact between forks
and containers immediately, even if no leak is visible.
