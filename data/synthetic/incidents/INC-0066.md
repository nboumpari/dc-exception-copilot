# INC-0066 - Inbound pallet(s) without SSCC barcode

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00102 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00103 | **SKU:** HBLC-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
1 of 1 pallets of HBLC-001 (Puro Household Bleach 6% Regular 3.78 L) on RCV-00103 from S-014 had no SSCC / GS1 logistic label.

## Timeline

- **2026-08-12 05:59** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-08-15 00:55** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label applied to stretch-wrap and torn off during transit.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Supplier quality notice issued; supplier asked for 8D corrective action report. Pallets quarantined at dock and relabeled with DC-generated SSCC after ASN reconciliation.
