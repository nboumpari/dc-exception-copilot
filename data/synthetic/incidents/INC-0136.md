# INC-0136 - Inbound pallet label barcode unreadable

**Site:** Meridian Consumer Goods - Columbus Distribution Center (DC-COL1)
**Exception:** EXC-00231 | **Severity:** Medium | **Status:** resolved
**Reference:** receipt RCV-00265 | **SKU:** DRNO-001 | **Zone:** D

## Summary
2 of 5 pallets of DRNO-001 (CleanPro Drain Opener Gel Max Gel 1 L) on RCV-00265 from S-014 had a GS1-128 SSCC barcode that failed to scan.

## Timeline
- **2026-09-16 06:14** - Exception detected and logged (receipt RCV-00265).
- **2026-09-16 06:14** - Kevin O'Brien (Receiving Lead) assigned as owner.
- **2026-09-16 07:31** - Corrective action completed; exception closed.

## Root cause
Label placed over a pallet wrap seam, causing wrinkles across the GS1-128 symbol.

## Corrective and preventive actions
Barcode verifier grade logged and sent to supplier. SSCC keyed manually from human-readable text after two-person verification.

- Regulations / standards: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline, GS1 General Specifications §5.4
