# Safety Data Sheet - Fresha Air Freshener Aerosol

## 1. Identification
- **Product:** Fresha Air Freshener Aerosol (250 mL)
- **Product family code:** AFRS
- **Recommended use:** Aerosol air freshener
- **Supplier:** Meridian Consumer Goods (fictional)

## 2. Hazard identification
**Signal word:** Danger

- Aerosol, Category 1 (H222: Extremely flammable aerosol)
- H229: Pressurized container - may burst if heated

## 4. First-aid measures
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. If drowsiness or dizziness occurs, get medical advice.
- **Skin:** Rinse with water. Remove contaminated clothing away from ignition sources.
- **Eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. If irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center.

## 5. Fire-fighting measures
Extremely flammable. Containers may rupture violently (projectile hazard) when heated. Use water spray, foam, dry chemical or CO2; cool containers from a distance.

## 6. Accidental release measures
Remove ignition sources. Ventilate. Collect leaking cans into a closed metal drum.

## 7. Handling and storage
Handle in accordance with good industrial hygiene. At DC-COL1 store only in zone H1
(Aerosol cage). Keep containers closed. Keep away from: Heat, sparks, open flames; strong oxidizers.

## 8. Exposure controls / personal protection
None for intact cans. Gloves when handling damaged cans.

## 9. Physical and chemical properties
Pressurized aerosol; liquid propellant; flammable.

## 10. Stability and reactivity
Stable under recommended storage conditions. **Incompatible materials:** Heat, sparks, open flames; strong oxidizers.

## 11. Toxicological information
Inhalation of high concentrations may cause dizziness.

## 12. Ecological information
Not expected to be harmful at use concentrations.

## 13. Disposal considerations
Dispose of via a licensed waste contractor in accordance with local, state and federal
regulations. Do not pour into drains.
